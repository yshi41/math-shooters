How to play

  • Catch the answer: the problem is at the top. Fly right up to the bubble with the right answer so your ship touches it, then press Space (CAPTURE on a touch screen) to catch it. You cannot catch from far away or by shooting. Bubbles you fly past are harmless, and astronauts are caught the same way.
  • Shoot the enemies: your ship fires on its own (turn Auto-fire off in Settings to shoot with left Ctrl or FIRE). Enemies glimmer just before they shoot, and a red arrow at the edge warns when something is flying in. Crashers fire and dive, saucers circle, bees come in swarms (down all five for a bonus), asteroids drift and split, and the Bubble Snatcher steals an answer bubble: shoot it to get the bubble back. Bullets never pop answer bubbles.
  • Power-ups: enemies drop capsules. Freeze stops everything, Time Warp slows it, Double Points doubles the score, Overdrive doubles your fire, Wingman adds a drone, and Shield, Laser Cell and Bomb help right away. They last a few seconds; the timers sit under your hearts.
  • Upgrades: after each wave pick one of three. They are yours to keep: they come with you to every world, and getting hit only costs a heart. Your ship shows them off: nose guns, wing guns, a glowing core, drones, missile rails. Max out two matching upgrades and a gold Evolution card appears.
  • Bonus Blitz: after wave 2, fifteen seconds where nothing can hurt you. Shoot all sixteen for a perfect, a power-up and a medal.
  • Astronauts and medals: three lost astronauts drift through every world; touch one and press Space to rescue it. Each world has four medals: Perfect, Untouched, Rescuer and Blitz. Every new medal is 10 treats.
  • Laser: it charges over time and faster with right answers. When your ship glows, press left Shift to burn a beam straight up for two seconds.
  • Bosses: the tank guards world 6 of the Addition, Times Tables and Division sectors and gets angry at half health. The Mothership guards the other sectors: shoot its two turrets, then the core. Every boss fights alone: nothing else comes in while it is up. The Math Hydra is a solo boss with its arms far apart. Its armor holds until you shoot off the arm with the right answer to the problem on its belly; then blast the body while it is open. Shoot a wrong arm and the numbers scramble and it grows stronger.
  • Controls: arrow keys or WASD to fly, Space to capture, left Ctrl to shoot, left Shift for the laser, B for a bomb, P to pause. On a computer the game goes full screen; Esc leaves it. On a touch screen put your right thumb anywhere on the right half: a ghost stick appears under it and the ship follows. LASER and BOMB sit bottom-left (FIRE too while Auto-fire is off). Swap the sides in Settings.
  • Two players: player 1 flies with WASD, shoots with F, lasers with left Shift and bombs with Q; player 2 flies with the arrows, shoots with . or right Ctrl, lasers with right Shift and bombs with /. Rescue a downed partner by flying into their bubble, take turns catching for Teamwork bonuses, and stay close so your beam pops enemy shots.
  • The map: each sector is one kind of math and gets harder world by world. Clearing world 3 of a sector opens the next sector. Mixed opens last.
  • Missions and Endless: three missions wait on this page; each pays treats and the next slides in when one is done. Clear any world 3 to open Endless: mixed problems, waves that never stop, a boss every fourth wave and a Blitz every fifth. How far can you get?
