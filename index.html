How to play

  • Catch the answer: the problem is at the top. Fly your ship into the bubble with the right answer. A missed bubble floats back up and comes around again.
  • Shoot the enemies: hold Space to fire (FIRE on a touch screen). Crashers dive at you, saucers shoot, and the tank guards world 6 of every sector. Bullets never pop answer bubbles.
  • Upgrades: after each wave pick one of three. Getting hit or catching a wrong answer costs a heart and knocks one upgrade loose: grab the floating capsule to get it back.
  • Controls: arrow keys or WASD to fly, Space to shoot, B for a bomb, P to pause. On a computer the game goes full screen; Esc leaves it. On a touch screen drag anywhere to fly and hold FIRE.
  • Two players: player 1 flies with WASD, shoots with F and bombs with Q; player 2 flies with the arrows, shoots with . and bombs with /. Rescue a downed partner by flying into their bubble, take turns catching for Teamwork bonuses, and stay close so your beam pops enemy shots.
  • The map: each sector is one kind of math and gets harder world by world. Clearing world 3 of a sector opens the next sector. Mixed opens last.
